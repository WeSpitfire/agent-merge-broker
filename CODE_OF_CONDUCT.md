# Code of conduct

Agent Merge Broker is committed to a respectful, useful, and harassment-free project community.
Participation includes issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release work, and other spaces
the maintainers represent as part of this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect differing experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Keep private information, credentials, and vulnerability details out of public reports.
- Accept a maintainer's request to pause, refocus, or leave a discussion.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, repeated
personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban a participant when needed
to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, and
the safety of people affected.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or maintainers. Include links and enough
context to investigate; do not start a public thread about the people involved. Maintainers will
limit disclosure to the people needed to respond and will avoid conflicts of interest where
practical.
