## Still required before `1.0.0`

- `agent-merge-broker-core` is **not published to npm**. The optional core
  publishing job was skipped. A maintainer must bootstrap it from a verified
  core artifact (rc.2 SHA-256 above, or the artifact of the final `1.0.0` release),
  configure its own trusted publisher, and verify its npm availability, dist-tag,
  package bytes, and provenance. Do not advertise registry installation of core
  until that is complete.
- An independent adopter must follow the published guide on the exact RC without
  implementation help and return a completed
  [walkthrough record](ADOPTER-TRIAL.md) with evidence links. The implementer's
  tests cannot self-certify this item.
- The upgrade-rehearsal limits recorded for rc.1 remain: idle registered tasks only,
  no in-flight legacy publication, pre-`0.12.0` data, old signing keys, or compressed
  historical archives were exercised. Follow the documented drain and full-runtime
  backup procedure before upgrades, or add those scenarios to the rehearsal before
  `1.0.0`.

Do not promote the RC or point npm `latest` at a 1.0 version while these required
acceptance items remain open. If the exact candidate changes, repeat the affected
checks and record a new candidate rather than editing the published tag.
